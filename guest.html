About Sosuke

Sosuke is the male protagonist of Ponyo. He lives on a cliff by the sea. His mother is a carer in a retirement home, and his father is a captain of a ship. He was the one to discover Ponyo. When he broke the glass in which Ponyo was stuck in, his finger was cut in the process. He is from a movie who gets to know a girl named Ponyo.
